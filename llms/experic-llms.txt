# Experic

> Experic is a specialist powder-handling contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) founded in 2018, headquartered in Cranbury, New Jersey, with an EU clinical supply center in Ireland. It provides pharmaceutical process, analytical and formulation development, clinical and commercial cGMP manufacturing, dry powder inhalation (DPI) and capsule/powder dosing, autoinjector and pen assembly, and clinical trial packaging, labeling, storage and logistics.
